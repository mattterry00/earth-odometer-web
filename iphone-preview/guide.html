Get started

  1. Open Journeys. Choose a walking or running journey, open its route details, and tap Start. Repeat with a driving journey if you want to follow both.
  2. Open Profile & settings → Tracking & permissions. Turn on the types of travel you want to record. Carry your iPhone on your first outing.
  3. Back on Home, tap Today to check the activity breakdown. Your on-foot and road journey cards show how much of each route you have covered.

Record a walk or run

  1. Turn on Automatic on-foot tracking and grant Motion & Fitness access when prompted. Walk or run with your iPhone. Open the app regularly to refresh the distance.
  2. For Health-based distance, turn on Use Apple Health distance and allow Walking + Running Distance. Recording starts when enabled. Earlier fitness history is outside your starting balance.
  3. To calibrate step-based estimates, turn off the Health distance option, measure 20 natural steps, then divide the total inches by 20. Turn on Use my step length and enter the result. It applies to future steps.

Record a drive or bike ride

  1. While parked, open Tracking & permissions. Turn on Automatic drive tracking or Automatic cycling tracking. Follow the location prompts and enable Always and Precise Location for background detection.
  2. To record a specific trip yourself, open Record a drive and tap Start drive before leaving and End drive after parking. The Last drive figure describes one trip. Today includes all recorded activities since local midnight.
  3. For a drive–walk–drive outing, carry the iPhone on each leg. Check Today afterward and compare Driving and On foot with their respective journey cards.

Correct the activity type

  1. Open Tracking & permissions → Correct activity type. Choose the date, the recorded activity, and the intended activity.
  2. Move all of the distance or enter the portion to reclassify. Tap Save correction. Check the day’s breakdown. Use Undo last correction for this day if needed.

Pause, resume, and finish a journey

  1. Open an active route and tap Pause to save its progress. Starting another route of the same type pauses the previous one. Your other active route can continue.
  2. Open View paused journeys, select the route and resume it. New matching distance advances it from the saved point.
  3. At the finish, open your stamp or choose your next journey. Extra distance stays in your travel totals. Tap the × on a Home notice to dismiss it. You can revisit the stamp in Passport.

Read totals and earn medals

  1. Home shows Today, This Year, and your lifetime odometer. Tap Today or This Year for activity details. Open Earth laps for distance around the planet.
  2. Open Medals to inspect a milestone and its distance. Marathon and similar medals recognize cumulative lifetime mileage across activities. Open Passport for checkpoints and finishes from virtual routes.

Share or save a card

  1. Open the item you want to share. From Today or This Year, choose the full breakdown or one activity. From Medals, open the specific medal first.
  2. Tap Share, choose Story or Feed, and wait for the image. Select Share image for the iPhone share sheet, or Save image to add it to Photos. Allow photo access if requested.
  3. Lifetime distance uses the odometer, recaps use the globe, journeys use destination artwork, and medal cards show the selected medal. Change miles or kilometers in Profile & settings.

Manage membership

  1. The ten shortest on-foot and ten shortest road journeys are free. Open Profile & settings → Membership to see your access.
  2. Choose Upgrade to Premium to view Apple’s subscription sheet and the price for your storefront. To recover an existing purchase, use Restore Purchases in that sheet with the Apple Account that purchased it.
  3. With an active subscription, open Membership → Manage subscription to review it.

Back up road progress

  1. Open Profile & settings → Backup & Restore. Choose a backup folder and tap Back Up Now. You can select a folder in iCloud Drive.
  2. To recover a backup, select Choose backup to restore, inspect its contents, then confirm. Unfinished road journeys resume in a paused state. Walking and running history are excluded.

If mileage is missing

  1. For a walk, check Automatic on-foot tracking and its data source, then tap Refresh on-foot distance. Check whether the iPhone traveled with you.
  2. For a drive, check location access and Precise Location. Check the day and activity in Travel history. Motorized transit can resemble driving.
  3. To report a problem, open Profile & settings → Report an Issue. Include the time, activity, expected distance, and the screen where the discrepancy appears. Review any screenshot before sending.

Follow a route in person

  1. On an iPhone build with in-person journeys, open Journeys. Under In-person journeys, choose Walk the Freedom Trail (Boston) or Drive Red Rock Canyon (Nevada). At the top of the route details, tap Walk this route in person or Drive this route in person to preview the mapped path and distance.
  2. Tap Directions to start beneath the map. On your first tap, choose Google Maps, Apple Maps, or Use iPhone default. Your choice opens directions to the Start marker and is saved for future routes on this iPhone. When you arrive, return to Earth Odometer.
  3. At the marked start, tap the walk or drive button. Start a driving route while parked. Allow Precise Location and Motion & Fitness. Walking starts on-foot recording, and driving uses the existing drive recorder. One start is enough.
  4. Follow the marked path in order. The orange marker shows the next GPS check. Motion & Fitness must recognize the matching activity. If GPS drops out, progress stays saved. Use Pause route during a break. After closing and reopening the app, open Passport → In-person stamps → Continue and tap Resume route.
  5. Complete the final check to earn your dated stamp. Open Passport → In-person stamps and select it to share the “I walked” or “I drove” card. Your virtual journey progress stays separate.

Frequently asked questions

When should I use Record a drive?

Use it to start and end a specific trip yourself while parked. If automatic drive tracking is already recording, leave it running. Starting Red Rock Canyon Scenic Drive in person also handles drive recording.

How do I choose or change my maps app?

Tap Directions to start and pick a maps app. Cancel saves nothing, so you can choose next time. To switch later, open Profile & settings → Preferred maps app and select another option. Selecting a preference here also skips the first-use prompt. Google Maps can open its web directions if the app is unavailable. Use iPhone default follows system navigation where available, with Apple Maps as the fallback.

Does getting directions start my in-person journey?

No. Directions to start opens navigation on your iPhone. To begin verifying checkpoints, return to the route in Earth Odometer and tap the walk or drive button at the Start marker.

Where do I find the Red Rock drive or Freedom Trail walk?

Open Journeys and look under In-person journeys. Choose the route, then tap the walk or drive button above its illustrated cover. On the website this button explains how to continue on iPhone. In the app it opens the route or shows membership options for a locked journey.

How does an in-person stamp differ from a virtual finish?

Virtual journeys use eligible miles wherever you travel. In-person stamps use precise GPS checks along the actual route and motion recognition. Imported miles, a photo, or a visit to the finish alone cannot earn one. Route verification never adds a second copy of your mileage.

What happens if I stop early or lose signal?

Completed checks stay on this iPhone. Resume from the next marked point. For a one-way drive, return on a future legal pass if needed. Discard route progress removes the unfinished attempt. Recorded mileage stays in your odometer.

Do I need to be in Boston to walk the Freedom Trail?

You can walk anywhere with your iPhone. Your recorded on-foot distance advances the virtual route. Open the route details to read about the real places you pass.

Why did my odometer move while my journey stayed still?

Check the activity and the route status. Walking and running advance an active on-foot journey. Driving advances an active road journey. A paused route keeps its saved distance. Cycling and flying can contribute to the odometer without advancing either route.

Does a paused banner mean everything has stopped?

Read the route name on the banner and look at the active cards below it. Dismiss the notice with its close button if you want to keep the active routes in view.

Why did only part of my walk appear on the route?

If you had 0.2 mi left on a route and recorded 1 mi, the final 0.2 mi completed it. Your daily and lifetime totals include the full mile. Choose another route before your next walk to start earning progress there.

Should Today equal Last drive?

Last drive shows one driving session. Today starts at local midnight and includes all recorded modes. Open Today on Home to compare activities from the same day.

Why is my run listed under On foot?

The iPhone groups short or uncertain segments with walking. In Tracking & permissions, open Correct activity type, select the day, and move the running portion to Running.

Does changing units affect my achievements?

Changing the display to kilometers converts distances on your dashboard and cards. The original medal keeps its milestone name and artwork.

Why do I have a marathon medal after several shorter outings?

The medal recognizes cumulative travel. Open the medal to see its threshold. Your virtual journey finish stamps record a different achievement.

How do I share only my running miles?

Tap Today or This Year, open its share card, and choose Running under Miles to share. Check the preview, choose Story or Feed, and tap Share image.

What should I check before restoring a backup?

Look at its date and the contents shown in the restore preview. It covers driving history, road journey progress, and distance units. Unfinished road routes return paused. Keep your existing backup until you have checked the restored totals.

Why does the website show different mileage?

The website uses sample travel. For your personal totals and earned items, open the installed app. A sample card identifies itself in its footer.

What can I do when a card image is missing?

Reopen the medal, journey, or recap and open Share again. Tap Try again if the artwork fails. If it repeats, report the exact item, Story or Feed format, your app build, and a screenshot.

What if I bought Premium but still see a lock?

Open Membership with the purchasing Apple Account. In the upgrade sheet, select Restore Purchases. Reopen Journeys after membership updates. In TestFlight, turn Preview Premium off when checking a sandbox purchase.

How do I get help without sending travel history?

Open Report an Issue and describe the screen, action, and unexpected result. A screenshot is optional. Review the draft and remove any information you prefer to keep private before sending.
