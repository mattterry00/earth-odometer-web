Start with your next mile.

Set up tracking once on your iPhone. Then pick where you want your miles to take you.

Turn on tracking

In Profile, open Tracking & permissions. Enable automatic walking or drive tracking and allow the permissions for each. For background drives, choose Always and Precise Location.

Choose your journeys

Open Journeys and pick an On Foot route and a By Road route. Tap Start on each. You can follow both at once, or simply keep adding to your lifetime miles.

Find your first stamp

Walk a mile and your on-foot journey advances. Drive a mile and your road journey advances. Check Passport for route stamps and Medals for lifetime milestones.

Your first outing, start to finish.

Follow a drive to the trail, a walk, and the drive home. See how to set up your iPhone, start an outing on your Watch, and check your miles afterward.

APPLE WATCH PREVIEW

Leave your phone.
Bring your miles.

Heading out with just your Watch? Start an outing before you pull away. Keep it running through the drive, the walk, and the ride home.

  1. Connect Apple Health on iPhone.

    Enable Automatic walking and Use Apple Health distance. Allow access to Walking + Running Distance so watch-only walks can count.

  2. Tap Start outing on your Watch.

    Allow precise location for road miles. Add Start outing to your watch face for one-tap access, or set an Outing reminder for a time you choose.

  3. End the outing when you get home.

    Reconnect the Watch and open Earth Odometer on your iPhone. Check both journeys. A 20-mile round trip and a 3-mile walk should add 23 miles to your lifetime total.

YOUR JOURNEY, YOUR DATA

Private by design.
Clear from day one.

Earth Odometer is being designed to keep recorded travel data on your iPhone, with optional iCloud syncing. Automatic distance tracking will require your permission. The app is still in development, and its final privacy disclosures will be published before launch.

What about miles before you install it? The app won't know your entire lifetime of travel automatically. Tracking starts when you begin using it; Apple Health distance begins when you enable it.
