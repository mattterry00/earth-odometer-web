YOUR JOURNEY, YOUR DATA

Private by design.
Clear from day one.

Earth Odometer is being designed to keep recorded travel data on your iPhone, with optional iCloud syncing. Automatic distance tracking will require your permission. The app is still in development, and its final privacy disclosures will be published before launch.

What about miles before you install it? The app won't know your entire lifetime of travel automatically. Tracking starts when you begin using it; any historical import capability will be explained separately if supported.
